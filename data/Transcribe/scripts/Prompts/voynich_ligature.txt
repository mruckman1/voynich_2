This analysis is Phase B.0 of a decipherment project. The specific
question is: does the EVA transcription system correctly identify

Your task is strictly physical observation. Do not attempt to
read, decode, or interpret any text. Do not use EVA character

WHAT TO RECORD:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

For each page, you will analyze the text lines only — not
marginalia, labels on illustrations, or star/plant labels.

SECOND: for each TEXT LINE on the page (ignoring illustration
labels and marginalia), examine every adjacent pair of marks
reading left-to-right.

For each adjacent pair, determine:

1. boundary_type
   Classify the physical boundary between the two marks:

   SEPARATED   — there is a clear gap or pen lift between
                 them; the two marks are visually independent
                 units with no connecting ink

   CONNECTED   — the ink flows continuously from one mark
                 to the next without a visible break; no
                 pen lift occurred between them

   WORD_SPACE  — the gap is large enough to constitute a
                 word space (the manuscript uses clear spaces
                 between words); record this for word boundary
                 positions so we can verify word segmentation

   AMBIGUOUS   — you cannot determine SEPARATED vs CONNECTED
                 with confidence from the image quality

2. mark_left_description
   Brief visual description of the LEFT mark of the pair,
   using only geometric terms: e.g. "small closed curve",
   "tall vertical stroke with descending hook", "looped
   figure-eight", "short diagonal". Do NOT use EVA names.

3. mark_right_description
   Brief visual description of the RIGHT mark.

4. line_number
   1 = topmost text line on the page.

5. position_in_line
   1 = leftmost pair in the line.

6. known_candidate
   If this pair matches one of the following high-frequency
   EVA bigrams that are candidates for being single signs
   (based on prior statistical analysis), flag it:
   "ch-candidate"   — the pair looks like what EVA calls "ch"
                      (a curved mark followed by a looped mark)
   "sh-candidate"   — similar to "ch" but the left mark
                      is taller
   "qo-candidate"   — a mark with a descender followed by
                      a small circle or oval
   "ai-candidate"   — two marks that could be "ai" or "aiin"
   "other"          — the pair is none of the above
   Assign this field based on visual appearance only, not
   on frequency data. If it doesn't resemble any candidate,
   use "other".

7. confidence
   "high"   — boundary is clearly visible, no ambiguity
   "medium" — some ambiguity but best judgment applied
   "low"    — image quality or damage prevents clear assessment
